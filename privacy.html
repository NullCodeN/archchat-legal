ArchChat Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Who we are

ArchChat is a messaging application developed and operated independently. We run our own server: messages, media and account data are stored there, not with third-party services.

2. What data we collect

Account

  • Username, tag, password, display name, profile picture, banner and status message.
  • Email and, optionally, a recovery email — used only to verify the account and recover your password/username. We do not ask for a phone number.
  • Account creation date and the last time you were online.

Content you send

  • Text messages, photos, videos, audio, files, stickers, GIFs, polls, stories and reactions.
  • Form responses, participation in raffles/cards and team records.

Location

  • Only when you choose to use it: when you send your location in a chat, share live location or trigger SOS, which sends your location to the emergency contacts you registered.
  • We do not collect background location unless you turned on one of these features.
  • Marketplace listings and job posts use only the city/state you type, not GPS.

Professional profile and Marketplace

  • Business name, category, description, address, hours, link, and the product listings and job posts you publish. This information is public inside the app.
  • Web page: if you turn it on, this same data, your picture/banner, listings and job posts appear on a public web page that can be found by search engines like Google. Turning it off removes the page immediately.

Other data you provide

  • Pix key, SOS emergency contacts, saved quick replies.

Technical data

  • Notification token, delivery/read status, which groups/channels you belong to.
  • App error reports (error message, app version and your user ID), so we can fix bugs.

What we do NOT collect: your phone's contact list, background location without your action, data from other apps, advertising identifiers.

3. How we use this data

Only to make the app work: delivering messages and calls, keeping history, authenticating login, recovering accounts, showing your profile to people you talk to, moderating reports and fixing bugs.

We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising. There are no ads in ArchChat and no marketing tracking.

4. Where data is stored and for how long

  • Messages and media stay on the server until you (or the group admin) delete them.
  • Disappearing messages are deleted automatically after the configured time.
  • Job posts expire on their set date; listings stay until you remove them.
  • Technical server backups are kept for up to 14 days and then deleted.
  • Error reports are used only to find and fix bugs.

5. Security

  • All communication between the app and the server uses encrypted HTTPS/WSS.
  • Messages and media are also encrypted at rest on the server.

No system is 100% secure; so we do not recommend sharing your data with other people (whether friends, family or spouse).

6. Reports and moderation

Reports of messages or users are stored with who reported, the reason and the reported content, for manual review.

7. Your rights

  • Delete your account: directly in the app, under Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate. What gets deleted is described on the "Account and data deletion" page.
  • Access and correct your data: name, photo, email, status and other profile data can be viewed and changed at any time in the app.
  • Block and report anyone, at any time.
  • Revoke permissions in Android settings whenever you want.
  • For any request about your data (including under Brazil's LGPD), use the contact email shown on the app's Google Play page.

8. Children

ArchChat is not intended for children under 13. We reinforce parents' duty and responsibility to supervise use.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Relevant changes will be announced inside the app, and the date at the top of this page will be updated.
